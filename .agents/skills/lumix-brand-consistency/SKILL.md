# Lumix brand and offer consistency

For Lumix marketing work, read `docs/seo/impulso-offer.json` from the repository root and check the current website and user's instructions. Treat its verification date as a snapshot: do not advertise a promotion after its expiry without an explicit new offer from the user.

- Use the correct business identity: Lumix Studio, Guatemala; website `https://lumixstudio.co/`; Instagram `@lumixstudio.gt`. Other similarly named domains or Instagram handles are not this business.
- Include a Lumix website destination and clear action in every caption/post deliverable unless the user specifically requests otherwise. Use a relevant page: web design, local SEO, contact, or the promotion. Instagram captions should print the correct URL and direct readers to the profile's clickable website link; do not imply a caption URL is always clickable.
- Use `https://lumixstudio.co/planes/#oferta-web` for the existing Impulso offer. If using campaign tracking, put query parameters before the fragment and keep the canonical destination unchanged.
- Distinguish the regular design/development price from the conditional promotional price. Keep discount arithmetic, consultation-reservation dates, new-customer eligibility and external-cost exclusions consistent. Do not turn a consultation-booking deadline into a purchase/payment deadline or promise permanent promotional pricing.
- Keep Crecimiento and Expansión as quotes by scope until the user approves fixed prices. Do not invent page limits, payment schedules, revision counts or client results.
- Verify the specific profile/account before editing or publishing. Publishing requires user authorization; a caption-writing request alone is a draft request. The skill itself grants no authority to publish, change security settings or modify unrelated profiles.
- Use appropriate fields for each platform. Google Business Profile's main description should describe the business; use service price fields, Offers and Posts for pricing/promotions. Preserve useful existing posts; correct inconsistent current offers rather than creating misleading duplicates.
- For a promotion post, include the price/eligibility conditions or a clear route to complete terms. For a general service post, do not force a promotion into unrelated content.
- Save a record of what was actually published and its destination. Distinguish submitted/pending review from publicly visible, and never claim that activity guarantees ranking or AI citations.
